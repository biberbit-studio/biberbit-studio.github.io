Privacy Policy – Odd Fish

Effective date: November 23, 2026 (updated the same day: in-app purchases)
Developer: BiberBit Studio
Contact: biberbitstudio@gmail.com

Odd Fish ("the App") is a find-the-odd-one-out game for Android. This policy explains what information the App handles and how.

Information the App stores on your device

The App saves your game progress locally on your device: best score, the zones and special modes you have opened, pearls, power-ups, the aquarium styles you own, the creatures in your Sea Book, achievements and your settings. This progress stays on your device unless you choose to connect Play Games (see below). Uninstalling the App deletes it. We operate no servers and receive none of this information.

Information the App does not collect

The App does not ask for your name, email, contacts, precise location, photos, microphone, or any account of its own. It has no chat and no user-to-user content. It contains no analytics service of ours.

Third-party services

The App uses the following services. Each has its own privacy policy, which governs the data it processes:

  • Google AdMob – shows ads (full-screen ads between levels and optional rewarded videos) and collects device identifiers (such as the Advertising ID), approximate location derived from your IP address, and diagnostic and ad interaction data for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device's Google settings. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.
  • Google Play Games Services (optional) – if you choose to connect your Play Games profile, Google identifies you by your Play Games player ID, and the App stores a copy of your game progress in your own Play Games cloud storage so you can restore it on another device. It also records the achievements you unlock. The App has no leaderboards. You can play the whole game without connecting.
  • Google Play Billing – processes in-app purchases. The App never sees your payment details. It keeps only a record on your device of the items you own and of the purchase tokens Google Play returns, so that each purchase is delivered exactly once; items that are kept for good can be restored on another device from your Google account. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play In-App Review – the App may ask Google Play to show its rating card. The rating and any review you write go to Google Play, not to us through the App.

Rate, Share and Feedback

These buttons in Settings only open other apps on your device: the Play Store page of the App, Android's share sheet with a ready message, or your mail app with a message addressed to us. Nothing is sent unless you send it yourself. If you write to us, we use your email address only to answer you.

Your choices

Where the law requires it, including in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the App asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change your answer at any time under Settings > Ad privacy choices. Buying Remove Ads stops the full-screen ads; rewarded videos stay optional and only play when you choose to watch one.

Children

The App is designed for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a third-party service, contact us and we will help remove it.

Permissions

The App uses these permissions:

  • VIBRATE – a short buzz on a mistake, which can be turned off in Settings.
  • INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE – loading ads, Play Games and purchases.
  • AD_ID and the Android ad services permissions (ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_TOPICS) – used by Google AdMob for ads and ad measurement.
  • WAKE_LOCK and FOREGROUND_SERVICE – added by Google's SDKs for their own background tasks. The App itself does not run in the background.

Data deletion

Gameplay data is stored on your device: Settings > Reset progress in the App, deleting the App, or clearing its storage in Android settings removes it. If you connected Play Games, you can delete the App's cloud save and your Play Games data for Odd Fish in the Play Games app under Settings > Delete Play Games account & data, or ask us at the address above. Purchase records are held by Google Play under your Google account. Data processed by Google for ads is governed by Google's policies linked above.

Changes

We may update this policy. The current version is always available at the URL listed in the App's store listing.
