Privacy Policy – CosmicPods

Effective date: November 14, 2026
Last updated: November 21, 2026
Developer: BiberBit Studio
Contact: biberbitstudio@gmail.com

CosmicPods ("the App", formerly named Cosmo Sort) is a puzzle game for Android. This policy explains what information the App handles and how.

Information the App stores on your device

The App saves your game progress locally on your device: levels completed, stars, coins, power-ups, settings and your Cosmic IQ skill scores. This progress stays on your device unless you choose to connect Play Games (see below). Uninstalling the App deletes it. Separately, the App sends anonymous usage statistics to GameAnalytics, as described under "Third-party services".

Information the App does not collect

The App does not ask for your name, email, contacts, precise location, photos, microphone, or any account of its own. It has no chat and no user-to-user content. If you choose to email us (see "Sharing, rating and feedback"), we receive what you send in that email.

Third-party services

The App uses the following services. Each has its own privacy policy, which governs the data it processes:

  • Google Play Billing – processes in-app purchases. The App never sees your payment details. It keeps only a purchase reference on your device so that an item is delivered once. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google AdMob – shows ads and collects device identifiers (such as the Advertising ID), approximate location derived from your IP address, and diagnostic and ad interaction data for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device's Google settings. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.
  • Google Play Games Services (optional) – if you choose to connect your Play Games profile, Google identifies you by your Play Games player ID, and the App stores a copy of your game progress in your own Play Games cloud storage so you can restore it on another device. It also records the achievements you unlock and the leaderboard scores you reach (levels beaten, stars, Cosmic IQ, daily streak). Leaderboard scores are visible to other players under your Play Games name, according to your Play Games profile settings. You can play the whole game without connecting.
  • GameAnalytics – receives anonymous usage statistics that help us balance the game: which levels are started, won or failed, which power-ups are used, when an ad is shown, and which in-app product was bought with its list price. Each report carries a random installation ID that the App creates on first launch (it is not your Advertising ID and is not linked to your Google account), your device model, Android version and the App's version. GameAnalytics also sees your IP address when the report arrives and uses it to estimate your country. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland these statistics are sent only if you agreed to storing information on your device in the consent form. See the GameAnalytics Privacy Policy.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase – in versions where it is enabled, collects anonymous usage events (for example which level was started, won or failed) together with a device identifier, to help us balance the game. It is not used to identify you.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the App schedules a few reminders on your device (daily gift, weekly event ending, a come-back note). They are created locally: no message is sent to our servers, and we operate no servers. You can switch them off in the App's settings or in Android's settings.

Sharing, rating and feedback

The Share buttons open Android's own share sheet with a short text about your result or an invitation with the App's store link. Nothing is sent unless you choose an app and send it yourself.

The Rate Us button opens the App's page in Google Play. Once in a while, after a win, the App may also show Google Play's own rating card. Your rating and review go to Google Play under your Google account; the App never sees them.

The Feedback button opens your own email app with a new message to biberbitstudio@gmail.com. Under the message it adds the App's version, your Android version, your device model and the App's language, so that we can look into what you report. Nothing is sent unless you send that email yourself. We use your email address and what you write only to answer you and to improve the App, and we delete your messages on request.

Your choices

Where the law requires it, including in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the App asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown and before usage statistics are sent. You can change your answer at any time under Settings > Privacy Choices in the App. Buying Remove Ads stops pop-up ads; rewarded videos stay optional and only play when you choose to watch one.

Children

The App is designed for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a third-party service, contact us and we will help remove it.

Permissions

The App uses these permissions:

  • VIBRATE – haptic feedback, which can be turned off in the App's settings.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS – the optional reminders described above. Android asks you first.
  • INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE – loading ads, purchases, Play Games and sending usage statistics.
  • BILLING – in-app purchases through Google Play.
  • AD_ID and the Android ad services permissions (ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_TOPICS) – used by Google AdMob for ads and ad measurement.
  • WAKE_LOCK, FOREGROUND_SERVICE and the install referrer permission – added by Google's SDKs for their own background tasks and measurement. The App itself does not run in the background.

Data deletion

Gameplay data is stored on your device: deleting the App, or clearing its storage in Android settings, removes it. If you connected Play Games, you can delete the App's cloud save and your Play Games data for CosmicPods in the Play Games app under Settings > Delete Play Games account & data, or ask us at the address above. Usage statistics are kept by GameAnalytics under the random installation ID only; clearing the App's storage or reinstalling it creates a new ID, and you can ask us at the address above to have statistics that were already sent deleted. Purchase records are held by Google Play under your Google account. Data processed by Google for ads is governed by Google's policies linked above.

Changes

We may update this policy. The current version is always available at the URL listed in the App's store listing.
