Privacy Policy – Bugs & Blocks: Garden Siege

Effective date: November 18, 2026
Developer: BiberBit Studio
Contact: biberbitstudio@gmail.com

Bugs & Blocks: Garden Siege ("the App") is a block puzzle game for Android. This policy explains what information the App handles and how.

Information the App stores on your device

The App saves your game progress locally on your device: best score, coins, power-ups, upgrades, the cosmetic items you own and your settings. This progress stays on your device unless you choose to connect Play Games (see below). Uninstalling the App deletes it. We operate no servers and receive none of this information. Separately, the App sends anonymous usage statistics to GameAnalytics, as described under "Third-party services".

Information the App does not collect

The App does not ask for your name, email, contacts, precise location, photos, microphone, or any account of its own. It has no chat and no user-to-user content.

Third-party services

The App uses the following services. Each has its own privacy policy, which governs the data it processes:

  • Google AdMob – shows ads and collects device identifiers (such as the Advertising ID), approximate location derived from your IP address, and diagnostic and ad interaction data for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device's Google settings. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.
  • Google Play Billing – in versions that offer in-app purchases, Google Play processes them. The App never sees your payment details. It keeps only a record on your device of the items you own. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Games Services (optional) – in versions that offer it, if you choose to connect your Play Games profile, Google identifies you by your Play Games player ID, and the App stores a copy of your game progress in your own Play Games cloud storage so you can restore it on another device. It also records the achievements you unlock and the leaderboard scores you reach (best score and highest stage). Leaderboard scores are visible to other players under your Play Games name, according to your Play Games profile settings. You can play the whole game without connecting.
  • GameAnalytics – receives anonymous usage statistics that help us balance the game: which stages are started, passed or lost and with what score, which creatures and features you have met, which power-ups are used, how coins are earned and spent inside the game, which achievements are earned, when an ad is shown, and which in-app product was bought with its list price. Each report carries a random installation ID that the App creates on first launch (it is not your Advertising ID and is not linked to your Google account), your device model, Android version and the App's version. GameAnalytics also sees your IP address when the report arrives and uses it to estimate your country. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland these statistics are sent only if you agreed to storing information on your device in the consent form. See the GameAnalytics Privacy Policy.

Your choices

Where the law requires it, including in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the App asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown and before usage statistics are sent. You can change your answer at any time with the Privacy Choices option on the App's home screen. Buying Remove Ads stops pop-up ads; rewarded videos stay optional and only play when you choose to watch one.

Children

The App is designed for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a third-party service, contact us and we will help remove it.

Permissions

The App uses these permissions:

  • VIBRATE – haptic feedback, which can be turned off on the App's home screen.
  • INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE – loading ads, purchases and Play Games, and sending the usage statistics.
  • AD_ID and the Android ad services permissions (ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_TOPICS) – used by Google AdMob for ads and ad measurement.
  • WAKE_LOCK and FOREGROUND_SERVICE – added by Google's SDKs for their own background tasks. The App itself does not run in the background.

Data deletion

Gameplay data is stored on your device: deleting the App, or clearing its storage in Android settings, removes it. If you connected Play Games, you can delete the App's cloud save and your Play Games data for Bugs & Blocks: Garden Siege in the Play Games app under Settings > Delete Play Games account & data, or ask us at the address above. Usage statistics are kept by GameAnalytics under the random installation ID only; clearing the App's storage or reinstalling it creates a new ID, and you can ask us at the address above to have statistics that were already sent deleted. Purchase records are held by Google Play under your Google account. Data processed by Google for ads is governed by Google's policies linked above.

Changes

We may update this policy. The current version is always available at the URL listed in the App's store listing.
