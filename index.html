02 About

A small studio that loves to build.

BiberBit is an independent studio. Like the beaver in our logo, we keep building, one game at a time, until it feels good to play.

What drives us is the moment a puzzle clicks: you pause, look again, spot the pattern and suddenly see three moves ahead. We build every level around that moment.

We believe a good brain game is a small workout for the mind, wrapped in something colourful enough that it never feels like homework.
  • Planning ahead
  • Pattern spotting
  • Memory
  • Focus
  • Patience
  • 01

    Thinking is the fun part

    The best moment in a game is when you figure something out. We design for that moment, not for mindless tapping.

  • 02

    A stretch for your logic

    Plan a few moves ahead, remember what you saw, stay focused under pressure: every level asks a little more of you.

  • 03

    Easy to start, deep to master

    Every game starts simple and teaches as you play, then keeps finding new ways to make you think.

  • 04

    Surprise keeps you sharp

    Bosses, new twists and daily challenges mean your brain never runs on autopilot.
