02 About

We build games the way beavers build dams.

Bit by bit, log by log, until it holds. It is right there in our name.

Step 5 / 5

How a beaver builds a game

  1. 01

    Pick the right tree

    A forest has thousands of trees, and a beaver picks only the special ones. Ideas are like trees: the real skill is choosing the one that turns into a game that makes your brain work, and makes that work fun.

  2. 02

    Gnaw it into shape

    A beaver gnaws, bite after bite, until the tree comes down. We build a rough first version and play it over and over, until it clicks: that moment you suddenly see the way through.

  3. 03

    Stack it, log by log

    A dam goes up one log at a time. A game goes up one level at a time: puzzles, bosses, sounds and secrets. CosmicPods alone has 1000 levels and 100 bosses, with 1000 more levels coming soon.

  4. 04

    Test the water

    A beaver checks every inch of its dam for leaks. Before a game comes out, real players test it, and every leak they find gets patched: a bug, an unfair level, a boring moment. Each one gets our full attention, until that moment is perfect.

  5. 05

    Make a pond

    When the dam holds, a pond forms and the whole forest comes to visit. Our games are that pond: a place you will want to come back to, for a few clever minutes every day.

And then? The teeth keep growing.
