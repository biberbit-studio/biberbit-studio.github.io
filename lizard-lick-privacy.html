Privacy Policy – Lizard Lick: Color Bug Hunt

Effective date: November 25, 2026
Developer: BiberBit Studio
Contact: biberbitstudio@gmail.com

Lizard Lick: Color Bug Hunt ("the App") is an arcade game for Android in which a chameleon catches bugs with its tongue. This policy explains what information the App handles and how.

Information the App stores on your device

The App saves your game progress and settings locally on your device: best score, highest stage, number of games played, totals such as bugs eaten and golden butterflies caught, your best streak, which achievements and awards were already unlocked, your daily missions and how many days you played, your best results in the daily hunt, the bugs in your bug book, the bugs you have to spend and the outfits you own and wear, your sound, music, vibration, language and accessibility settings, which packs you own, and when the App last asked you to rate it. This information stays on your device unless you choose to connect Play Games (see below). Uninstalling the App deletes it. We operate no servers and receive none of this information. Separately, the App sends anonymous usage statistics to GameAnalytics, as described under "Third-party services".

Information the App does not collect

The App does not ask for your name, email, contacts, precise location, photos, camera, microphone, or any account of its own. It has no chat and no user-to-user content. Apart from the anonymous usage statistics described below, it reports nothing about how you play.

Third-party services

The App uses the following services. Each has its own privacy policy, which governs the data it processes:

  • Google AdMob – shows ads and collects device identifiers (such as the Advertising ID), approximate location derived from your IP address, and diagnostic and ad interaction data for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device's Google settings. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.
  • Google Play Billing – processes the optional purchases (the dragon sets, the outfit packs and No Pop-up Ads; earlier also the Supporter Pack). The App never sees your payment details. It keeps only a record of what you own, and Google Play restores it on a new device. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Games Services (optional) – if you choose to connect your Play Games profile, Google identifies you by your Play Games player ID, the App stores a copy of your game progress in your own Play Games cloud storage so you can restore it on another device, and the achievements you unlock are recorded in your Play Games profile. The App has no leaderboards. You can play the whole game without connecting.
  • GameAnalytics – receives anonymous usage statistics that help us balance the game: which stages are started, passed or lost, with what score and what ended the hunt, the first steps of a new player, which forest and which boss the hunt reached, the daily hunt and its score, which missions and awards are completed, how bugs are earned and spent, which screens, shop items and packs are opened or tried on, when the share button is used, when you keep going after a game over (with a video or for free), when an ad is shown, which achievements are earned, whether you connected Play Games, and the purchase of a pack with its list price. Each report carries a random installation ID that the App creates on first launch (it is not your Advertising ID and is not linked to your Google account), your device model, Android version and the App's version. GameAnalytics also sees your IP address when the report arrives and uses it to estimate your country. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland these statistics are sent only if you agreed to storing information on your device in the consent form. See the GameAnalytics Privacy Policy.

Sharing, rating and feedback

  • Share opens Android's share sheet with a ready message and, when you share from the end of a game, your score and a picture of your chameleon that the App draws itself. The picture contains no photos of yours and nothing personal, and it is kept only in the App's private storage, where the next one replaces it. You choose the app and the people it goes to; the App itself sends nothing.
  • Rate us opens the App's page in the Google Play Store. After a new best score the App may also ask Google Play to show its rating card, at most three times and no more than once a month. Ratings and reviews are handled by Google.
  • Feedback opens your own email app with a draft addressed to us. The draft includes the App's version, your phone model and your Android version, which help us fix problems; you can see and edit everything before sending, and nothing is sent unless you send it. If you write to us, we receive your email address and your message, use them only to answer you and improve the game, and delete them on request.

Your choices

Where the law requires it, including in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the App asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown and before usage statistics are sent. You can change your answer at any time with Privacy Options in the App's Settings. No Pop-up Ads and the Diamond Dragon set (and the earlier Supporter Pack) stop pop-up ads; rewarded videos are always optional and only play when you choose to watch one.

Children

The App is designed for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a third-party service, contact us and we will help remove it.

Permissions

The App uses these permissions:

  • VIBRATE – haptic feedback, which can be turned off in the App's Settings.
  • INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE – loading ads, purchases and Play Games, and sending the usage statistics. The game itself can be played offline.
  • com.android.vending.BILLING – Google Play purchases.
  • AD_ID and the Android ad services permissions (ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_TOPICS) – used by Google AdMob for ads and ad measurement.
  • WAKE_LOCK and FOREGROUND_SERVICE – added by Google's SDKs for their own background tasks. The App itself does not run in the background.

Security

The Google services and GameAnalytics send their data over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Your local progress is kept in the App's private storage on your device.

Data deletion

Gameplay data is stored on your device: deleting the App, or clearing its storage in Android settings, removes it. If you connected Play Games, you can delete the App's cloud save and your Play Games data for Lizard Lick: Color Bug Hunt in the Play Games app under Settings > Delete Play Games account & data, or ask us at the address above. Usage statistics are kept by GameAnalytics under the random installation ID only; clearing the App's storage or reinstalling it creates a new ID, and you can ask us at the address above to have statistics that were already sent deleted. Purchase records are held by Google Play under your Google account. Data processed by Google for ads is governed by Google's policies linked above.

Changes

We may update this policy. The current version is always available at biberbit.com/lizard-lick-privacy.html, the address listed in the App's store listing.
